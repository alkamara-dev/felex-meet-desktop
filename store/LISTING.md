**Description:**
FELEX Meet is a video meeting app for small businesses, made by FELEX Universal Services in Winchester, Virginia.

Every person gets their own permanent meeting link. Share it once and use it every day. Guests join from the link with just their name: they do not need an account or an install.

What you can do:
- Start a meeting in your own room, or schedule one and send calendar invites that open in Outlook or Google Calendar.
- Share your whole screen or a single window in high definition. This app includes its own screen and window picker.
- Turn on live captions, raise your hand, react, and chat during a meeting.
- Blur or replace your background and filter out background noise.
- Run team chat, and keep your meetings and team in one workspace.
- Move a meeting from your computer to your phone: if you are already in a meeting on another device, you can move it to this one, or join from both.
- Sign in with your email, a passkey, an authenticator app, or Google.

This Windows app opens FELEX Meet in its own window with its own taskbar icon, and handles screen sharing and notifications natively. Your meetings, account and team are the same as on meet.felexuss.com in any browser and on your phone.

FELEX Meet is free to use while its Pro features are free, with no credit card needed. See meet.felexuss.com for current plans and limits.
